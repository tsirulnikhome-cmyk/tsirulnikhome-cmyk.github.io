Privacy policy

Home Admin Mail is used only by its owner, on his own computer, with his own Google account.

  • It reads the owner's Gmail messages and attachments to organize receipts and, when the owner asks, creates drafts, sends messages or applies labels.
  • Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
